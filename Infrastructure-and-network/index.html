Reference guide

How to play

Goal

Reach the finish line with energy above zero and the highest score you can get.

Controls

  • ← → or A D — change lanes
  • W or ↑ — boost (cooldown bar)
  • S or ↓ — brake
  • Space — horn / vehicle action (same as clicking the game)
  • Esc or Backspace — pause · same keys to resume
  • Touch: swipe left/right to steer · swipe up for boost · tap screen halves to move

Road items

  • Puddles & rival cars — hazards, dodge or lose energy.
  • Stars +100 · Gems +150 — score pickups.
  • Shield — absorbs one hit.
  • Quiz Boost — answer a life-skills question for a speed burst.
  • Super Streak — 3 correct answers → 8s of 1.2× score & pickup magnet.
  • Second Chance — answer after a hit to recover energy.

Signs along the track

Each track has friendly billboards with life-skills reminders. Click one to read the tip and earn +500 bonus points the first time.
